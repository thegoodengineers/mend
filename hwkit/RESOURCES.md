(proc, flatten, opt, simplemap). Flip-flop counts are exact. That Yosys has no `synth` or `techmap`,
so adders, comparators and wide muxes stay as word-level cells; they are listed by type instead of
being turned into a guessed gate count. This is not an FPGA LUT count.
